Privacy policy

Syllabus · last updated 7 November 2026

What Syllabus accesses

When you sign in with Google, Syllabus asks for access to your Google Calendar and Google Tasks. It uses that access only to show your calendars, events and tasks in the app, and to send back the changes you make in it (new, edited or deleted events, tasks and calendars, and calendar colours).

Where your data goes

Your calendar data is kept on your own Mac, in the app's folder in Application Support. Your Google sign-in is kept in your Mac's keychain. Syllabus sends your data only to Google's own servers, to sync it. It has no servers of its own, and it shares, sells or transfers your data to no one else. It collects no analytics.

Google user data

Syllabus's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Sign out in Syllabus (Calendars → Google), or remove Syllabus at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's folder removes everything it kept on your Mac.
