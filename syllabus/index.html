Syllabus

A calendar for school, for the Mac.

Syllabus is a personal calendar app. It shows your days, weeks and months, keeps your course links and PDFs at hand, and syncs your Google Calendar events and Google Tasks so you can see and change them on your Mac.

It is a personal project by its developer, made for their own use. It is not sold or distributed to the public.
